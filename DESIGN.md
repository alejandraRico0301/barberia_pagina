## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a refined digital flagship for a prestige grooming atelier in Pereira, Colombia. It bridges the time-honored ceremony of classic bespoke barbering with razor-sharp contemporary luxury. 

The aesthetic is tailored, commanding, and impeccably curated. Rather than relying on vintage tropes (like distressed wood or literal barber pole motifs), it projects a five-star hospitality atmosphere: obsidian marble, warm brushed brass, fine sartorial tailoring, and intimate studio lighting. The user experience evokes confidence, precision, and VIP discretion—appealing to clients who regard self-care as an essential ritual of personal standard.

The core visual language unites high-end tactile minimalism with dark editorial glamour: ultra-clean structural layouts, deep layered blacks, subtle gold metallic leaf accents, hairline brass dividers, and generous breathing room.

## Colors

The palette revolves around deep atmospheric tones, layered gold metallurgy, and crisp ivory contrasts:

- **Surface Foundations:**
  - `Base Canvas`: `#0B0B0B` (pure nocturnal depth, absorbing light).
  - `Surface Level 1` (Cards / Panels): `#141414` (rich obsidian).
  - `Surface Level 2` (Elevated modals / popovers): `#1C1C1C` (refined charcoal tint).
  - `Surface Inset` (Fields / inputs): `#080808`.

- **Gold Acentos & Highlights:**
  - `Primary Gold`: `#D4AF37` (classic imperial gold, reserved for primary CTAs, active states, and focal icons).
  - `Muted Brass`: `#C5A059` (satin antique gold for subtle micro-borders, metadata, and badge strokes).
  - `Gold Glow / Sheen`: `#DFBA54` (illuminated gold for hover highlights and delicate glows).

- **Typography & High Contrast:**
  - `Text Primary`: `#FFFFFF` (pure optical white for crystalline legibility).
  - `Text Secondary`: `#F5F5F5` (soft alabaster for body copy and narrative sections).
  - `Text Tertiary / Muted`: `#9E9E9E` (neutral ash for inactive states, labels, and timestamps).
  - `Border Hairlines`: `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.18)` and `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.

## Typography

The typography couples the editorial heritage of classic European barber saloons with sleek modern Swiss discipline:

- **Headlines (`Bodoni Moda`):** High-contrast vertical stems, razor-fine serifs, and architectural drama. Used for impactful headers, prestige service names, pricing figures, and quotes. Evokes master craftsmanship and bespoke perfumery.
- **Body & Controls (`Manrope`):** Balanced, geometric, and functional with exceptional legibility across dark backgrounds. It grounds the drama of Bodoni Moda, ensuring booking workflows, timeslots, and technical haircut specs are effortlessly digestible.
- **Eyebrows & Editorial Labels (`label-caps`):** Rendered in uppercase with generous letter tracking (`0.14em`) and medium brass coloration to serve as chapter markers and categorization tags.

## Layout & Spacing

The design system employs a 12-column responsive fluid grid structured around balanced negative space, allowing luxury items and services room to breathe without clutter.

- **Desktop (>= 1200px):** 12-column grid, max canvas width 1280px centered, 48px (`margin`) outer margins, 24px (`gutter`) column gaps.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column grid, 32px outer margins, 20px column gutters.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column grid, 20px (`margin-mobile`) outer margins, 16px column gutters. Vertical spacing relies on generous 40px–64px section separation to prevent claustrophobia in dark mode.

Vertical rhythm adheres to an 8px baseline. Dense content stacks are broken up with delicate brass rules (`1px` height, fading horizontally).

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is treated with cinematic restraint through tonal layers, brushed hairline outlines, and warm golden luminescent glows rather than conventional muddy drop shadows:

- **Level 0 (Canvas):** Pure `#0B0B0B`. The background substrate.
- **Level 1 (Cards & Static Containers):** Solid `#141414` bordered with `1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`. No shadow needed at rest.
- **Level 2 (Hovered Cards & Interactive Modules):** Shift to `#1A1A1A` with border brightening to `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.35)` accompanied by a subtle gold ambient halo: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Floating Menus, Booking Sheets & Drawers):** Solid `#1C1C1C` with backdrop-filter blur (`16px blur`) on glass underlays, framed with `1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.25)` and a dual shadow: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8)`, `0 0 20px 0 rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.06)`.
- **Hairline Accent Lines:** Key dividers use linear gradient fills from `transparent` to `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.4)` to `transparent` to mimic the light reflection on a polished straight razor.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a tailored, architectural radius (`roundedness: 1` — Soft / 4px base). 

- Standard inputs, buttons, and badges utilize subtle 4px to 6px radii. This slight rounding softens the technical feel without compromising masculine, structured geometry.
- Large cards, booking dialogs, and service banners use 8px (`rounded-lg`).
- Fully rounded pills are strictly reserved for service category tags, availability indicators, and interactive status badges.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (The Gold Ingot):** Linear gradient fill `linear-gradient(135deg, #DFBA54 0%, #D4AF37 50%, #C5A059 100%)`, text in `#0B0B0B` (bold `label-md`), subtle gold rim glow `0 4px 16px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.25)`. On hover, brightness amplifies with `0 6px 20px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.40)`.
- **Secondary (Tuxedo Outline):** Transparent fill, 1px border in `#D4AF37`, text in `#FFFFFF`. On hover, surface becomes `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.08)` and border illuminates to `#DFBA54`.
- **Ghost:** Transparent fill, text in `#F5F5F5` with subtle underline appearing on hover in `#D4AF37`.

### Cards & Service Tiles
- Background `#141414`, framed by `1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.14)`.
- Header typography in `Bodoni Moda` with price tags styled in `title-lg` gold typography.
- Barber profile and portfolio cards feature a subtle gradient overlay across imagery (`linear-gradient(to top, #141414 15%, transparent 80%)`).

### Chips & Filter Pills
- Inactive: Background `#0B0B0B`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, text `#9E9E9E`.
- Active: Background `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.12)`, border `1px solid #D4AF37`, text `#FFFFFF`, accompanied by a tiny 4px circular gold indicator pip.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Background `#080808`, height 48px, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, font `#FFFFFF`.
- Focus state: Border transitions smoothly to `#D4AF37` with an ambient glow ring `0 0 0 3px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`.
- Placeholder text in muted `#666666`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Custom square/circle with deep black surface `#080808` and `1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.3)`.
- Checked state: Solid `#D4AF37` background with a pitch-black `#0B0B0B` checkmark or interior dot.

### Specialized Barber Components
- **Time Slot Selector:** Compact modular grid buttons displaying appointment times. Unavailable slots rendered in low-opacity `#333333` strikethrough; selected slot highlighted in solid gold with dark numbering.
- **Service Tier Ribbon:** Distinctive editorial card with a corner brass crest indicating signature rituals (e.g., "Ritual Barba & Toalla Caliente").
